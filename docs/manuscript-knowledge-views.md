# Manuscript knowledge views

The Universe's completed book view separates Characters, Story & plot, and Reader appeal & marketing. This is a presentation of saved, unreviewed extraction results; it does not re-read, modify or regenerate the manuscript.

Characters group normalized names and unique explicit aliases matching another saved name within the same manuscript version. Shared nicknames and fuzzy spelling do not join profiles. The shortest recorded name labels a joined profile; other names remain searchable aliases. Spoiler filtering occurs before grouping and search so hidden identity information cannot affect visible names or joins. These automatic groups are not author-approved identities. Original observations, including potentially conflicting ones, retain their sources under Role, About, Personality, Relationships, Story development and Possible marketing angle.

Story observations sharing their first cited passage appear in one selectable passage group. This is not semantic scene detection, a scene timeline or a claim that every finding in a scene has been combined. Findings citing other passages remain separately inspectable. Source quotes and original records are unchanged.

Reader appeal groups saved genre, theme, trope, tone, reader-promise, content, hook and comparable-title findings. Source support and inference labels remain visible. It provides a suggested Ask Raven question for audience brainstorming; it does not claim research into actual reader demand or automatically run a paid analysis.

Character search matches saved visible names and aliases locally without spending credits. Full manuscript search remains available separately. Tests cover identity grouping, ambiguous aliases, spoiler filtering, source retention, view separation, private source dialogs, desktop/mobile accessibility and manuscript replacement.

## September 20 UX review and redesign

The review found repeated summary text, oversized cards, nested disclosures and no clear next action. The interface now uses a compact searchable index and one focused reading panel. Character Organization offers At a glance, Relationships, Development and Reader appeal; Story Arc preserves passage grouping without claiming chronological scenes; Marketing explains how each kind of evidence can inform a small experiment. Content findings lead to reader-care notes, not automatic advertising hooks.

Each selection offers an editable Raven question or a saved next step on Cassandra’s Desk. Questions carry the selected facet or matching observations and the book selection, and never submit automatically. An unfinished Raven draft is protected from replacement. Desk notes retain book, manuscript and passage references and remain unapproved working notes. These actions do not change the manuscript or write fiction.

Supporting passages open directly. Exact duplicate display text combines source references without deleting original observations. Search reveals matching observations before pagination, including matches deep in a group. Keyboard focus, mobile return-to-index navigation, spoiler filtering and explicit source/inference labels remain available.
